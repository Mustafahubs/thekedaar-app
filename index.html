Everything a thekedaar writes in the register, now automatic.

Site crews and daily attendance

Add your masons and labourers to a site once. They stay there, so each morning you only tap full day, half day or absent.

  • One crew per site, remembered every day
  • A worker can be on more than one site, never more than one full day
  • See who is working where, and each worker’s week so far

Thursday hisaab without the arithmetic

The week runs Saturday to Friday. On Thursday the app adds up every worker’s days, takes off advances, and shows what each person is owed, per site.

  • Mason and labourer daily rates, per site if you want
  • Advances tracked and deducted automatically
  • A Thursday bill as a PDF, ready to share on WhatsApp

A measurement calculator that thinks in feet and inches

Type length and width in feet and inches. Need to take out a staircase or an open area? Switch on the cut and it subtracts for you.

  • Roof, wall, floor, tile and marble, each with its own rate
  • Many parts in one job; finished parts fold into small rows
  • Save measurements to a site and attach a voice note

Bills for the owner

Turn site measurements into a clean PDF bill and send it from the app.

Worker profiles

Photo, joining date, time worked, total earned, advances and payment history.

Safe on one phone

Your account is active on one phone at a time, and your records are backed up when you are online.

How to install

  1. Tap Download above and wait for the file to finish.
  2. Open the downloaded file from your notifications or the Downloads folder.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps from this source, then go back and tap Install.
  4. Open Thekedaar App and log in with your WhatsApp number.

The app is not on the Play Store, so Android or Play Protect may show a warning for apps from outside the store. That is normal for a file you install yourself. Only download from this page.

Questions

Do I need internet to use it?

Only to log in the first time, and to back up your records. After that you can mark attendance, measure and settle offline. Everything syncs when you are back online.

Can I use it on two phones?

One phone at a time. If you log in on a new phone, the old one is logged out. Your records come with you, because they are backed up.

What if the WhatsApp code does not arrive?

Wait for the timer, then tap Resend. You can also link an email or your Google account in Profile as a backup way to log in.

Which languages does it support?

Urdu and English. You can switch in Profile at any time.

What data do you keep?

Your login number, and the records you enter. Nothing is sold or shared. Read the full privacy policy.
